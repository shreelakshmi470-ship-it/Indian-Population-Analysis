India is the most populous country in the world, with an estimated population exceeding 1.47 billion people. Accounting for roughly one-sixth of the global population, it continues to experience a moderate growth rate of under 1% annually, driven primarily by a large proportion of young citizens. This project is about the population distribution in India, collected in the Census 2011. It has data about the state wise, district wise and subdistrict-wise number of households, total population, total workers etc.
